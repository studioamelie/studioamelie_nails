THE RIGHT FIT

Sizing made simple.

A good fit is what makes press-ons look polished and wear properly.

TWO WAYS TO SIZE

Choose your fit.

01 · FREE

Photo + coin

Photograph both hands from directly above with a €1 or £1 coin beside your nails on the same flat surface. After checkout, we will email you with instructions for sending the photos.

Coin sizing is an estimate and isn't always accurate. Camera angle and perspective can affect the result.

02 · €4

Sizing kit

I send you a sizing kit so you can try the nail tips and tell me the exact size you want for each finger.

The kit is estimated to arrive in about 4 days. After you email me your chosen sizes, the finished set is estimated to arrive about 4 days later. These are estimates and international delivery may take longer.

How to order: choose your sizing method in the bag. For the kit, the €4 charge is added once to your order. Your shipping option and total are shown at checkout before payment.

ABOUT STUDIO AMÉLIE

Small studio. Big nail energy.

Handmade, not mass produced.

Studio Amélie Nails is built around fashion-led press-ons that feel personal. Every set is made by hand, with permanent signatures alongside seasonal drops and fully custom designs.

The idea is simple: detailed, salon-style nails you can wear without arranging an appointment — with the freedom to choose a design and fit that actually feels like you.

HELP & CARE

Everything you need to know.

What comes with my set?

Your press-on order is designed to include the essentials for prep and application. Final kit contents can be confirmed before launch.

Are the nails reusable?

Yes. Careful application and removal can help you reuse your handmade set.

How do custom orders work?

Use Create Your Set to choose your options. After ordering, reply to our email with your inspiration photos. Contact us before paying if you need details beyond the listed options.

How do I choose my size?

Choose either coin photos (an estimate that may be inaccurate) or a €4 sizing kit to try on. The kit typically arrives in about 4 days; after you tell me your chosen sizes, your set is estimated to arrive about 4 days later. International delivery can take longer. See the sizing guide.

How do I pay and when will it arrive?

Choose your delivery country and shipping method at checkout, then pay securely by card through Stripe. Delivery estimates can vary.
